## Abstract

::: {.callout-note title="Abstract Logic"}
Before the reader commits, they must see the **D**efinition and **R**esources. They need the **I**ndicator to trust the result, the **V**ariation to see the nuance, and the **E**xplanation to understand the 'Why'."
:::

::: {.callout-important title="Word Limit: 200--250 Words"}
- **D (Define):** 1-2 sentences. What is the topic and tool?
- **R (Resources):** 1 sentence. What data/time range?
- **I (Indicator):** 1-2 sentences. What is the MAIN number?
- **V (Variation):** 1 sentence. Where did it differ?
- **E (Explanation):** 1 sentence. Why did it happen?
:::

**Rule:** No references, no abbreviations (unless defined), no vague statements.

---

#### D - DEFINE (The Hook)

_The Abstract is not a summary; it is a sales pitch. It must drive the reader to the full text._

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Immediately link your **Subject** with your **Lens/Method**.

**Template:** *The present study assesses [Topic] using [Tool/Sensor].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _This study assesses the spatiotemporal variance of Urban Heat Island (UHI) intensity across the city using multi-temporal Landsat TIRS thermal data._
- _This research monitors forest fragmentation dynamics and canopy density variations using a dense Sentinel-1/2 time-series analysis._
- _This paper analyzes flood susceptibility and identifies high-risk zones using the AHP method integrated with Sentinel-1 imagery._
- _We assess agricultural drought severity and duration using long-term MODIS Vegetation Condition Index (VCI) time-series._
- _The study quantifies urban sprawl dynamics and predicts future expansion patterns using Cellular Automata prediction models._
:::

---

#### R - RESOURCES (The Input)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
What raw material did you process? (Data, Texts, Code, Patients). Specify the **Raw Material** and **Interval**.

**Template:** *[Sensor] images from [Time Range] were processed to derive [Metric].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _Landsat-5 and Landsat-8 images spanning the period 2000–2024 were processed to derive consistent Land Surface Temperature (LST) layers._
- _Multi-decadal Landsat and Sentinel-2 composites covering 1990–2024 were utilized to calculate and track NDVI trend indices._
- _Sentinel-1 SAR data from the years 2018–2024 was pre-processed to map the recurring seasonal inundation extents._
- _Long-term MODIS and CHIRPS precipitation datasets spanning 2000–2024 were analyzed to continually monitor vegetation health._
- _Landsat 5/8 and Sentinel-2 imagery from 1990–2024 were used to perform supervised land cover classification and change detection._
:::

---

#### I - INDICATOR (The Main Result)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
The single most important change or finding. State the black **Quantitative Shift**.

**Template:** *Results indicate that [Key Metric] [Increased/Decreased] by [Amount].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _Results indicate that the mean LST has increased by 2.4°C, showing a strong positive correlation ($R^2=0.82$) with built-up density._
- _Analysis reveals that the dense core forest area has declined by 15% due to the inward propagation of edge effects._
- _The maximum inundation extent was found to have increased by 22% ($p<0.05$), particularly affecting low-lying administrative wards._
- _The mean Vegetation Condition Index (VCI) dropped below 35, signaling the specific onset of severe agricultural stress._
- _Built-up areas were observed to have expanded by 45%, consuming vast tracts of prime agricultural land in the process._
:::

---

#### V - VARIATION (The Nuance)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
The contrast. Nothing is uniform. Show the **Spatial Contrast** or Nuance.

**Template:** *Spatial profiling shows that [Zone A] degraded most, while [Zone B] remained stable.*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _Spatial profiling reveals that the industrial northwest exhibited the highest warming rate (+0.4°C/year), whereas the old cantonment remained stable._
- _Degradation peaked in accessible low-lying valleys, while the inaccessible high-altitude ridges remained relatively intact._
- _Riverine zones faced prolonged water stagnation, whereas highland areas drained effectively within 6 hours of peak rainfall._
- _Rainfed districts demonstrated high vulnerability, in contrast to canal-irrigated zones which remained relatively stable during dry spells._
- _Ribbon development dominated along major highways, contrasting with the partial, scattered growth observed in the rural fringe._
:::

---

#### E - EXPLANATION (The Takeaway)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
The attribution or deeper meaning. Provide the **Attribution** or Policy Link.

**Template:** *This trend is primarily driven by [Cause], overriding the influence of [Secondary Factor].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _This warming trend is primarily driven by unchecked industrial expansion, overriding the cooling influence of local water bodies._
- _The observed fragmentation is largely driven by the expansion of linear infrastructure, which has fragmented the contiguous habitat._
- _The increased flood risk is attributed to the systematic constriction of natural drainage channels by recent urban encroachment._
- _This chronic stress pattern is driven by the increasing variability of monsoon rainfall, exacerbating soil moisture deficits._
- _This rapid expansion is driven by speculative real estate investment, which outpaces the actual population growth requirements._
:::

#### Case Study Abstracts

::: {.callout-note title="UHI"}
This study assesses the spatiotemporal variance of Urban Heat Island (UHI) intensity across the city using multi-temporal Landsat TIRS thermal data. Landsat-5 and Landsat-8 images spanning the period 2000–2024 were processed to derive consistent Land Surface Temperature (LST) layers. Results indicate that the mean LST has increased by 2.4°C, showing a strong positive correlation ($R^2=0.82$) with built-up density. Spatial profiling reveals that the industrial northwest exhibited the highest warming rate (+0.4°C/year), whereas the old cantonment remained stable. This warming trend is primarily driven by unchecked industrial expansion, overriding the cooling influence of local water bodies.

**Breakdown:**
- **D (Define):** This study assesses the spatiotemporal variance of Urban Heat Island (UHI) intensity across the city using multi-temporal Landsat TIRS thermal data.
- **R (Resources):** Landsat-5 and Landsat-8 images spanning the period 2000–2024 were processed to derive consistent Land Surface Temperature (LST) layers.
- **I (Indicator):** Results indicate that the mean LST has increased by 2.4°C, showing a strong positive correlation ($R^2=0.82$) with built-up density.
- **V (Variation):** Spatial profiling reveals that the industrial northwest exhibited the highest warming rate (+0.4°C/year), whereas the old cantonment remained stable.
- **E (Explanation):** This warming trend is primarily driven by unchecked industrial expansion, overriding the cooling influence of local water bodies.
:::

::: {.callout-note title="Forest"}
This research monitors forest fragmentation dynamics and canopy density variations using a dense Sentinel-1/2 time-series analysis. Multi-decadal Landsat and Sentinel-2 composites covering 1990–2024 were utilized to calculate and track NDVI trend indices. Analysis reveals that the dense core forest area has declined by 15% due to the inward propagation of edge effects. Degradation peaked in accessible low-lying valleys, while the inaccessible high-altitude ridges remained relatively intact. The observed fragmentation is largely driven by the expansion of linear infrastructure, which has fragmented the contiguous habitat.

**Breakdown:**
- **D (Define):** This research monitors forest fragmentation dynamics and canopy density variations using a dense Sentinel-1/2 time-series analysis.
- **R (Resources):** Multi-decadal Landsat and Sentinel-2 composites covering 1990–2024 were utilized to calculate and track NDVI trend indices.
- **I (Indicator):** Analysis reveals that the dense core forest area has declined by 15% due to the inward propagation of edge effects.
- **V (Variation):** Degradation peaked in accessible low-lying valleys, while the inaccessible high-altitude ridges remained relatively intact.
- **E (Explanation):** The observed fragmentation is largely driven by the expansion of linear infrastructure, which has fragmented the contiguous habitat.
:::

::: {.callout-note title="Flood"}
This paper analyzes flood susceptibility and identifies high-risk zones using the AHP method integrated with Sentinel-1 imagery. Sentinel-1 SAR data from the years 2018–2024 was pre-processed to map recurring seasonal inundation extents. Results indicate that the maximum inundation extent was found to have increased by 22% ($p<0.05$), particularly affecting low-lying administrative wards. Spatial profiling shows that riverine zones faced prolonged water stagnation, whereas highland areas drained effectively within 6 hours. The increased flood risk is attributed to the systematic constriction of natural drainage channels by recent urban encroachment.

**Breakdown:**
- **D (Define):** This paper analyzes flood susceptibility and identifies high-risk zones using the AHP method integrated with Sentinel-1 imagery.
- **R (Resources):** Sentinel-1 SAR data from the years 2018–2024 was pre-processed to map recurring seasonal inundation extents.

- **V (Variation):** Spatial profiling shows that riverine zones faced prolonged water stagnation, whereas highland areas drained effectively within 6 hours.
- **E (Explanation):** The increased flood risk is attributed to the systematic constriction of natural drainage channels by recent urban encroachment.
:::

::: {.callout-note title="Drought"}
We assess agricultural drought severity and duration using long-term MODIS Vegetation Condition Index (VCI) time-series. Long-term MODIS and CHIRPS precipitation datasets spanning 2000–2024 were analyzed to continually monitor vegetation health. Results indicate that the mean VCI dropped below 35, signaling the specific onset of severe agricultural stress. Rainfed districts demonstrated high vulnerability, in contrast to canal-irrigated zones which remained relatively stable during dry spells. This chronic stress pattern is driven by the increasing variability of monsoon rainfall, exacerbating soil moisture deficits.

::: {.callout-note title="Urban Sprawl"}
The study quantifies urban sprawl dynamics and predicts future expansion patterns using Cellular Automata prediction models. Landsat 5/8 and Sentinel-2 imagery from 1990–2024 were used to perform supervised land cover classification and change detection. Results indicate that built-up areas were observed to have expanded by 45%, consuming vast tracts of prime agricultural land in the process. Ribbon development dominated along major highways, contrasting with the partial, scattered growth observed in the rural fringe. This rapid expansion is driven by speculative real estate investment, which outpaces the actual population growth requirements.

#### k-e-y-s (Style extras)

::: {.callout-note title="Micro-Checklist"}
- **k (Keywords):** Are the 5 references searchable?
- **e (Economy):** Is every word necessary? (Kill adjectives).
- **y (Yield):** Does it yield a clear result?
- **s (Standalone):** Can it survive without the paper?
:::

#### Final Checklist

::: {.callout-important title="Pre-Submission Check"}
- [ ] **Word Count:** Is it between 200-250 words?
- [ ] **D:** Is the Tool/Method mentioned in the first sentence?
- [ ] **R:** Are the specific dates and data sources listed?
- [ ] **I:** Is there a number (percentage, $p$-value, quantity)?
- [ ] **V:** Is there a comparison (High vs Low, Core vs Periphery)?
- [ ] **E:** Is the 'Why' (attribution) clearly stated?
- [ ] **Tone:** Are there any vague words? (Remove: "very", "some", "good").
:::
