## Title and Keywords

::: {.callout-note title="Title Logic"}
Most natural titles flow as **P $→$ S $→$ C $→$ O $→$ E**. *"[Precision] of [Subject] in [Context] using [Operation] for [Effect]."*

**Spatiotemporal Assessment** of **Forest Loss and Climatic Impacts** in **Similipal Biosphere Reserve** using **Geospatial Trend Analysis** for ...
:::

::: {.callout-important title="Word Limit: 12--18 Words"}
- **P (Precision):** What is the specific lens? The academic lens (e.g., Spatiotemporal Assessment).
- **S (Subject):** What is the main topic? The core topic (e.g., Urban Heat Island).
- **C (Context):** Where is it happening? The location/system (e.g., in Bhubaneswar).
- **O (Operation):** How was it done? The method/tool (e.g., using TIRS Data).
- **E (Effect):** What was found? The utility/outcome (e.g., for Urban Planning).
:::

---

#### P - PRECISION (The Lens)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Add a **Qualifier**. Is it Spatiotemporal? Comparative? Predictive?

**Template:** *A [Adjective] Analysis...*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _...A **Spatiotemporal** Analysis..._
- _...A **Comparative** Assessment..._
- _...A **High-Resolution** Approach..._
- _...A **Multi-Decadal** Evaluation..._
- _...A **Simulation** Approach..._
:::

---

#### S - SUBJECT (The Core)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Place the most important noun **First** or **Second**.

**Template:** *[Core Subject] Assessment...* or *Mapping [Core Subject]...*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- **Urban Heat Island (UHI)** Intensity...
- **Forest Fragmentation** Dynamics...
- **Flood Susceptibility** Zoning...
- **Agricultural Drought** Monitoring...
- **Urban Sprawl** Patterns...
- **Land Use/Land Cover** Changes...
- **Groundwater Potential** Zones...
- **Coastal Vulnerability** Trends...
- **Air Quality (PM$_{2.5}$)** Spatio-Temporal Variations...
- **Glacial Lake** Outburst Floods...
- **Soil Erosion** Severity...
- **Wetland Ecosystem** Dynamics...
- **Crop Health** Vigor...
- **Seismic Hazard** Microzonation...
- **Solid Waste** Landfill Suitability...
:::

---

#### C - CONTEXT (The Setting)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Define the **Geography** or **System**. Avoid "in India" if possible; be specific.

**Template:** *...in the [Specific Region/City]...* or *...across [Biome Type]...*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _...across the **Rapidly Urbanizing Fringe of Bhubaneswar**..._
- _...in the **Tropical Deciduous Tracts of Similipal**..._
- _...along the **Mahanadi River Basin**..._
- _...in **Rainfed Districts of Western Odisha**..._
- _...for the **Metropolitan Region**..._
:::

---

#### O - OPERATION (The Method)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Name your **Primary Tool**. This flags the paper for methodologists.

**Template:** *...using [Tool/Algorithm]...* or *...integrated with [Model]...*
:::

::: {.callout-important title="Advanced: The \"Hidden Data\" Strategy"}
**The Insight:** Standard datasets (like Landsat, Census) are often assumed or secondary.
- **Mention Data if:** It is unique, novel, or the main selling point (e.g., "First use of SWOT Satellite...").
- **Hide Data if:** The *Method* is the hero. Replace "using Landsat" with "using A Spatial Econometric Approach" or simply omit the "using..." phrase if the Context implies the method.
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _...using **Multi-Temporal Landsat TIRS Data**..._
- _...using **Dense Sentinel-1/2 Time-Series**..._
- _...using **AHP Strategy and SAR Imagery**..._
- _...using **Long-term MODIS VCI Indices**..._
- _...using **Cellular Automata Prediction Models**..._
:::

---

#### E - EFFECT (The Outcome - Optional)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Hint at the **Utility**. What is this for?

**Template:** *...for [Application/Policy].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _...for **Sustainable Urban Planning**._
- _...for **Conservation Prioritization**._
- _...for **Disaster Risk Reduction**._
- _...for **Crop Yield Optimization**._
- _...for **Smart City Development**._
:::

#### Capitalization in Titles

::: {.callout-note title="Standard Conventions"}
**Title Case (Most Common):**
- Capitalize the **First** and **Last** word.
- Capitalize all **Major** words (Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs, Pronouns). 
- **Do NOT** capitalize minor words (Articles: a, an, the; Conjunctions: and, but, or, nor, for, yet, so; Prepositions: in, on, at, to, of, by) unless they start the title.

**Sentence Case (Alternative):**
- Capitalize **Only** the first word and Proper Nouns (names of specific places, organizations, or people).

**Tip:** Most detailed academic studies (like this framework) default to **Title Case** for maximum impact.
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Examples of Capitalization" collapse="true"}
- **Correct Title Case:** *"**S**patiotemporal **A**ssessment **o**f **U**rban **H**eat **I**sland **U**sing **G**eospatial **T**echniques"* (Note: Verbs like "Using" and Nouns are capitalized; Prepositions like "of" are lowercase).
- **Correct Sentence Case:** *"**S**patiotemporal assessment of urban heat island using geospatial techniques"* (Note: Only the first word is capitalized).
- **Handling Colon (:)** *"**U**rban **F**looding: **A** **C**ase **S**tudy **o**f **M**umbai"* (Rule: Always capitalize the first word after a colon).

**The Narrative:**

"The **S**ubject is explored within a **C**ontext using a specific **O**peration. We focus with **P**recision to reveal the **E**ffect."
"To build the Title, we define the **P**recision (Lens) of the **S**ubject, place it within its **C**ontext, apply the **O**peration (Method), and imply the final **E**ffect."
:::

#### Guidelines: The Colon Strategy

::: {.callout-note title="Format & Capitalization Rules"}
The "Colon Style" is favored by high-impact journals (Nature, Science) because it front-loads the main topic.

**1. The Structure:**
- *[Broad Subject]: [Specific Study/Method]*
  - **Example:** *Urban Heat Island: Spatiotemporal Analysis...* (Not "Spatiotemporal Analysis of Urban Heat Island...")

**2. Capitalization Rules:**
- **Before Colon:** Use Title Case.
- **After Colon:** **ALWAYS** capitalize the first word, even if it is a minor word (a, an, the).
- **Example:** *...Impacts: **A** Case Study...* (Correct) vs *...Impacts: **a** Case Study...* (Incorrect).
:::

#### Guidelines: The Keyword Strategy

::: {.callout-note title="Selection Rules"}
Keywords are the hashtags of academia.
- **Hierarchy:** Organize from Broad Domain to Specific Method.
- **Discovery:** Distinct from the Title. (If 'Remote Sensing' is in the title, use 'Earth Observation' here).
- **Limit:** Standard is 5--6 phrases.
- **T (Topic):** The Broad Domain (e.g., Remote Sensing).
- **A (Area):** The Geography (e.g., Eastern India).
- **G (Grain):** The Scale/Resolution (e.g., Time-Series).
- **S (Specifics):** The Method/Theory (e.g., Random Forest).
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Winning Tactics (The Dos)"}
- **Do use Synonyms:** If your title says "Urban Heat", use "Thermal Comfort" or "Microclimate" as keywords.
- **Do Include Methods:** Always include the black tool/method (e.g., "AHP", "Random Forest", "Ethnography", "NDVI").
- **Do Test Searchability:** Ask: "If a researcher types this keyword, is my paper exactly what they are looking for?"
:::

::: {.callout-important title="Critical Mistakes (The Don'ts)"}
- **Don't Repeat:** Do not just copy words from the Title. (Title: "Forest Fire" $→$ Keyword: "Forest Fire" = Wasted Opportunity).
- **Don't be Generic:** Avoid single words like "Analysis", "Study", or "India". Be specific ("Time-Series Analysis", "Odisha Coastal Zone").
- **Don't use Abbreviations:** Avoid "LST" or "NDVI" alone unless they are universally standard.
:::

#### T-A-G-S (Style extras)

::: {.callout-note title="Micro-Checklist"}
- **T (Title):** Is it under 18 words?
- **A (Acronyms):** Are acronyms defined or standard?
- **G (Gap):** Does it imply a gap/solution?
- **S (Search):** Are keywords distinct from the title?
:::

::: {.callout-important title="Pre-Submission Check"}

- [ ] **Key 1:** Is the Method in the Title?
