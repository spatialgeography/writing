## Discussion

::: {.callout-note title="Discussion Logic"}
We make a **C**laim based on our **O**bservation. We define the **D**ifference from past work, admit the **E**rror, and finally prove the **S**ignificance.
:::

::: {.callout-important title="Word Limit: 1200--1500 Words"}
- **C + O (The Confirmation):** (400 words). State the answer and point to the evidence (Figure/Table).
- **D (The Debate):** (600 words). Compare with 5-10 other papers. "Our results align with X, but contradict Y because..."
- **E + S (The Reality):** (400 words). Admit limitations honestly, then pivot to the policy impact.
:::

**Rule:** Every paragraph should reference a Figure (e.g., "As seen in Fig 3...").

_CODE_

---

#### C - CLAIM (The Answer)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Directly answer the question posed in the Introduction.

**Template:** *We unequivocally claim that [Phenomenon] is driven by [Factor].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _Our findings substantiate the claim that the observed local warming is primarily driven by industrial land-use patterns rather than broader climatic shifts._
- _We demonstrate that the fragmentation of the reserve is being accelerated by mining infrastructure, which acts as the black driver of degradation._
- _The study establishes that the recurrent flooding is fundamentally a morphological issue, exacerbated by the artificial confinement of the river by embankments._
- _We argue that the region's crisis is distinctively an agricultural drought, triggered by the delayed onset of the monsoon rather than total rainfall deficit._
- _The analysis confirms that the urban expansion is largely speculative, driven more by anticipated future road networks than by current population pressure._
:::

---

#### O - OBSERVATION (The Evidence)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Highlight specific data points that support the claim.

**Template:** *This is substantiated by [Data Point/Observation].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _This is evidenced by the stark 4°C thermal differential observed between the distinct heat islands in paved zones and the cooling islands in parklands._
- _Observationally, this is confirmed by the 60% vegetation loss recorded within the mine buffer zones, compared to less than 10% in non-mining areas._
- _This is clearly supported by the data showing that major embankment breaches consistently occurred at high-stress meander bends during peak flow._
- _The strongest evidence for this is the high correlation between the timing of the monsoon arrival and the severe dip in crop yield indices._
- _This observation is validated by the 'leapfrog' growth pattern, where new developments bypass existing greenbelts to cluster around proposed highway interchanges._
:::

---

#### D - DIFFERENCE (The Comparison)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Compare with previous work. Do you agree or clash?

**Template:** *This finding [Aligns with/Contradicts] previous studies by [Author].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _This result stands in contrast to studies on coastal cities, as the mitigating influence of the sea breeze is noticeably absent in this inland configuration._
- _Unlike the plantation-driven loss seen in the Western Ghats, the degradation here is uniquely linked to the linear intrusion of extractive transport networks._
- _Our results differ from standard upstream models by highlighting that in this deltaic region, the 'tidal lock' effect is the dominant flood mechanism._
- _This finding contradicts purely meteorological drought assessments, which often classify the season as 'normal' based on total volume alone._
- _Unlike Western urban models that follow concentric growth, this region exhibits a chaotic, mixed-use sprawl characteristic of rapid developing economies._
:::

---

#### E - ERROR (The Limitation)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Be honest about weaknesses.

**Template:** *However, [Limitation] introduces potential uncertainty.*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _However, we acknowledge that limiting the analysis to a 10:30 AM satellite overpass captures the heating phase but misses the daily maximum temperature._
- _A limitation of this study is the reliance on 30m resolution imagery, which may overlook small-scale selective logging activities under the canopy._
- _It is important to note that the vertical error margin of 1m in the DEM limits the precision of the depth estimation in micro-topography._
- _However, the spatial resolution of 250m prevents us from distinguishing between rainfed plots and those with private tube-well irrigation._
- _A potential source of error remains the spectral confusion between bare soil in construction sites and seasonal fallow land in satellite imagery._
:::

---

#### S - SIGNIFICANCE (The Impact)

::: {.callout-note title="Rule"}
Why does this matter for the future?

**Template:** *These findings are significant for [Policy/Future Research].*
:::

::: {.callout-tip title="Example Sentences"}
- _Crucially, these findings provide the quantitative evidence needed to enforce 'Cool Roof' codes and 'Green FSI' policies in the upcoming master plan._
- _Significantly, this maps the critical breakage points in elephant migration corridors, offering a blueprint for urgent protective status designation._
- _The immediate implication is that future flood management must prioritize embankment strengthening at identified weak points rather than generic dredging._
- _Consequently, this research provides the empirical basis necessary for designing parametric crop insurance payouts based on onset delays._
- _There is a critical need to use these findings to justify strict 'Green Belt' enforcement before the remaining wetlands are irreversibly converted._
:::

However, the satellite overpass time of 10:30 AM limits the night-time analysis. These findings are significant for enforcing 'cool roof' regulations.

We unequivocally claim that forest fragmentation is driven by unchecked linear infrastructure expansion. This is substantiated by the high linearity index of degradation patterns. These findings are significant for designating eco-sensitive zones.

We unequivocally claim that flood intensification is driven by drainage channel encroachment. This is substantiated by the 40% reduction in channel width observed in satellite imagery. These findings are significant for redesigning the city's storm water master plan.

These findings are significant for designing parametric crop insurance products.
:::
